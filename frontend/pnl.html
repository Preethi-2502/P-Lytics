Understanding Your P&L

Realised P&L

Profit or loss from shares that have already been sold. FIFO is used to determine the cost of the sold shares.

Unrealised P&L

Profit or loss from shares that are still held in the portfolio, based on the current market price.

FIFO

FIFO means First In, First Out. The oldest available BUY lot is matched with a SELL first.

Current Holdings P&L

Unrealised P&L for shares currently held.

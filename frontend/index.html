How P&Lytics Works

P&Lytics simplifies portfolio analysis by processing broker contract-note data automatically.

📄

1. Upload PDF

Upload your broker contract note containing your trading transactions.

🔍

2. Extract Trades

The PDF parser extracts stock, date, quantity, price, and transaction details.

🔄

3. FIFO Matching

Buy and sell transactions are matched using the First In, First Out method.

📊

4. Calculate P&L

The system calculates realised and unrealised profit or loss for your portfolio.
